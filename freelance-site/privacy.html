Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026 · Questions: setup@handoff.app

Handoff ("Handoff", "we") provides a tool that packs files and delivery details into a single shareable web link, sold at stampofapproval.lol. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site, and the limited ways we use it.

What we collect

  • Account/contact email. When you buy Pro or email yourself a link, Stripe shares your payment contact email with us so we can deliver and support your purchase.
  • Payment data. Card details never touch our servers — payments are processed by Stripe. We store only payment identifiers and metadata needed to operate your subscription, unlock paid features, and revoke access after a refund or chargeback.
  • Portal content. The titles, links, and descriptions you put in a portal are stored to make the service work. Don't paste anything into a portal you wouldn't be comfortable sending to your client.
  • Server logs. Standard hosting logs (IP address, timestamps, pages requested) kept for security and troubleshooting.
  • Measurement. We use the Meta (Facebook) pixel on this site to measure page views and purchases, and to understand which of our own ads led to a sale. This involves cookies or similar identifiers set by Meta. You can block it with any cookie blocker or browser setting, or opt out of Meta's use of this data via Meta's ad-preference and opt-out tools (for example, the Digital Advertising Alliance's WebChoices tool).

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We don't email you marketing you didn't ask for.
  • We don't ask your clients for accounts to open a portal — a portal is a plain link with no login.

Service providers

  • Stripe — payment processing (PCI-DSS).
  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery (Cloudflare Pages).
  • Meta Platforms — advertising measurement (pixel).

Data retention & deletion

Free portal data is kept while the link is in use. If you want your data deleted, email setup@handoff.app from your account email and we'll remove it. Payment records are kept as long as required for tax and dispute purposes.

Children

Handoff is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
